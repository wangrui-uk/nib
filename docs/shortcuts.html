Shortcuts

ShortcutWhat it does
⌥⌥ tap Option twiceCorrect the selection, or the whole field if nothing is selected
⇧⇧ tap Shift twiceRewrite the selection (or click the ✨ button that appears at the end of a selection)
⌘⌘ tap Command twicePause or resume Nib. While paused, nothing is read or sent.
⌃⌥ZUndo the last correction, rewrite or applied fix

In the live popup

⇥ TabApply this fix
⇧⇥ Shift-TabApply every underlined fix in the field
escIgnore this suggestion for the session

These keys are only captured while the popup is showing for the field you're in. Otherwise Tab and Escape reach your app as normal.

In the suggestion and rewrite cards

↩Accept / Replace
⌘RTry again (a different rewrite)
escDismiss

How double-taps work

A double-tap counts only when the key is tapped on its own: two short presses close together, with no other key, modifier or click in between. Typing with ⌥, ⇧ or ⌘ (⌘C, ⌘Tab, ⌥-arrows…) never triggers it. Prefer regular shortcuts? In Settings → Shortcuts choose Custom shortcut and record one.
