FAQ

Is Nib really free?
Yes, MIT-licensed, with no paid tier. You pay your AI provider for what you use; with Flash-Lite-class models, everyday writing typically costs very little. A local model costs nothing.
How much am I using?
Settings → Usage shows calls and tokens for today, 7 days or 30 days, by feature and by model, as your provider reported them. Use your provider's console for prices. The statistics stay on your Mac.
Does my text go to a Nib server?
No. There's no Nib server. Text goes from your Mac directly to the provider you chose. See Privacy.
Which provider should I pick?
Gemini Flash-Lite through Google AI Studio is the default: fast, inexpensive and good at this task. Claude Haiku and GPT mini models work well too. For full privacy, use a local model through Custom (OpenAI-compatible).
Does it work offline?
Only with a local model (Ollama, LM Studio) set up as a Custom provider.
Which languages?
Nib keeps your language and your spelling variety (British or American English). How well other languages are checked depends on the model.
Will it change my formatting?
In native apps, Nib writes back only the changed words, so bold, links and lists are kept. In Chromium and Electron apps ⌥⌥ pastes plain text, so select just the sentence if formatting matters.
Can I hide macOS's own red underlines or autocorrect bubbles?
Yes, with opt-in switches in Settings → Live checking. Your settings are restored when you turn them off, pause or quit. Writing Tools can only be turned off in System Settings → Apple Intelligence & Siri.
Why does Nib need Accessibility?
It's how macOS lets an app read the field you're typing in, find where words are on screen, and write corrections back.
How do updates work?
Nib uses Sparkle. Updates are signed and notarised. Nib checks automatically (you can turn this off), or use Check for Updates… in the menu.
How can I help?
Report bugs with a Copy Diagnostics report, suggest apps to support, or send a pull request: see CONTRIBUTING.md.

Nib is free and open source (MIT). No account, no Nib server, no analytics. Source · Issues · Changelog · Report a vulnerability
