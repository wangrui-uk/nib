Setup

  1. Install

    Download the DMG from the latest release, open it, and drag Nib to Applications. Then open Nib. Its nib icon appears in the menu bar, and the setup guide opens.

  2. Allow Accessibility

    Nib reads and fixes text in other apps through macOS Accessibility. In the setup guide click Open Accessibility Settings and turn on Nib. The status turns green within a second.

    If Nib is listed but still shows “Not allowed” (this can happen after an update or a rebuild), remove it with −, add it again, and click Restart Nib in the setup guide.

  3. Choose a provider and paste your key

    Pick a provider, paste its API key, click Load models and pick a model, then Test connection. Keys are stored in your login Keychain, one per provider.

  4. Read the privacy note and confirm

    Nib explains what will be sent where. Live checking starts only after you confirm. You can change this any time in Settings → Privacy.

Settings worth knowing

  • Languages I write in: which languages are checked as you type (default: English plus your Mac's languages). Each paragraph's language is detected on your Mac; Chinese and Korean get their own rules (wrong characters and measure words; spacing, particles and speech level).
  • Live checking: on or off, per-app off list (Turn Off in <App> in the menu), and experimental underlines in code editors.
  • Correction: apply straight away, or show the changes first; an extra instruction such as “use British English”.
  • Appearance: popup font and size.
  • macOS text features: opt-in switches to hide macOS's own spelling underlines, autocorrect or predictive text while Nib is on. Your settings are restored when you turn them off, pause or quit.
  • Usage: calls and tokens for today, 7 or 30 days, by feature and model; Export CSV; an optional “Today: N calls · X tokens” line in the menu. Kept on your Mac only.
  • Updates: check automatically (on by default), or Check for Updates… in the menu.
