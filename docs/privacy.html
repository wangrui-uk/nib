Privacy

Nib has no server, no account, no analytics and no tracking. Your text goes from your Mac straight to the AI provider you choose, with your own key.

What is sent, and when

Live checkingAbout a second after you stop typing, the paragraphs Nib hasn't seen yet in the field you're typing in, one paragraph at a time. In VS Code, only the visible lines of prose files.
Correct (⌥⌥)The selected text, or the whole field.
Rewrite (⇧⇧ / ✨)The selected text and the style's instruction.
Paused (⌘⌘)Nothing.

Nib asks you to confirm before live checking runs for the first time. You can turn live checking off entirely, or for one app (Turn Off in <App>).

What is never read

  • Password fields.
  • Terminals (Terminal, iTerm2, Warp, Ghostty, kitty and others).
  • Apps you've turned off.

Where things are stored

  • API keys: in your login Keychain, one item per provider. Never written to disk in plain text, never logged.
  • Settings: in Nib's preferences on your Mac.
  • Logs: ~/Library/Logs/Nib.log records the app, the method used, lengths, timings and errors. Never your text.
  • Usage statistics (Settings → Usage): time, feature, provider, model and token counts per call, in ~/Library/Application Support/Nib/usage.json. They stay on your Mac, are never sent anywhere and never contain text. You can export them as CSV or reset them.
  • Copy Diagnostics reports numbers and settings only (roles, positions, which step failed), never text.

Your provider

Each provider handles the text it receives under its own terms, so check your provider's data policy. To keep everything on your Mac, run a local model (Ollama, LM Studio) and choose Custom (OpenAI-compatible).

Updates

Nib checks this site's update feed (appcast.xml) once a day by default. That request carries no text and no identifiers beyond what any web request does. Updates are signed (EdDSA) and notarised by Apple. You can turn automatic checks off in Settings.

The source code is public, so you can check all of this: https://github.com/wangrui-uk/nib.
